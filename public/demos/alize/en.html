Our Services

Pioneering Sustainable Tech

Four integrated capabilities, one team. We handle feasibility, engineering, financing and operation — so your transition never stalls between two service providers.

Solar Integration

Rooftop, ground-mount and carport arrays engineered for your site — from the feasibility study through to grid connection.

Wind Energy

Energy Storage

Battery systems that hold what you generate and release it when tariffs peak — typically paying for themselves in under six years.

Eco-Manufacturing

About Us

A decade spent making clean energy the boring option

Alizé was founded by four grid engineers who were tired of watching good climate projects die in spreadsheets. We took the parts everyone finds painful — modelling, permitting, financing, operation — and turned them into one accountable process.

  • One team, end to end

    The engineers who model your site are the ones who operate it afterwards.

  • Measured, not promised

    Every installation ships with live metering and a public performance dashboard.

  • Financing included

    Power purchase agreements from 8 years, so capital expenditure never blocks the decision.
